About Dr Walker

A senior psychologist who takes complex presentations seriously.

I've spent over two decades working with people whose difficulties don't fit neatly into a six-week programme. My clinical work is grounded in Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy (ISTDP) — an evidence-based approach with strong outcomes for complex presentations.

Beyond her doctorate, Dr Walker completed core ISTDP training under Dr Stephen Arthey in Melbourne, followed by advanced training in Norway with Dr Patricia Coughlin. She is an accredited ISTDP therapist and teacher — not only practising this approach, but training other clinicians in it.

She works from Robina on the Gold Coast, with telehealth available across Australia and internationally.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out.

Finding the right psychologist is an important decision. These are the questions I hear most often.

Book a Consultation
You don't need a GP referral to see me as a private patient. However, if you have a Mental Health Treatment Plan (MHTP), you may be eligible for Medicare rebates on up to 10 sessions per year. I'm happy to discuss what works best for your situation.
Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy (ISTDP) is an evidence-based approach that works with unconscious emotional processes underlying psychological difficulties. It has strong empirical support for complex presentations — personality disorders, treatment-resistant depression, anxiety — and addresses root causes rather than managing symptoms.
The first session is a comprehensive assessment — typically 90 minutes — where I gain a thorough understanding of your history, current difficulties, and goals. It's also an opportunity for you to assess whether working with me feels right. There's no pressure to commit to ongoing sessions.
International sessions are via a secure, encrypted telehealth platform. I schedule early mornings and evenings to accommodate clients in the UK, Europe, and North America. Payment is via international bank transfer or card.
Standard 50-minute sessions are $280. Initial assessment sessions (90 minutes) are $420. Medicare rebates apply for patients with a valid Mental Health Treatment Plan, significantly reducing out-of-pocket costs. I'm transparent about fees from the outset.
