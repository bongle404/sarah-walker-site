About Dr Walker

A senior psychologist who takes complex presentations seriously.

I've spent over two decades working with people whose difficulties don't fit neatly into a six-week programme. My clinical work is grounded in Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy (ISTDP) — an evidence-based approach with an established track record for complex presentations.

My training spans clinical psychology, psychotherapy, and specialist work with eating disorders, trauma, and personality disorders. I am an ISTDP-accredited therapist and teacher, which means I not only practise this approach — I train other clinicians in it.

I work from the Gold Coast, with in-person sessions available at Robina and telehealth sessions for clients across Australia and internationally.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out.

I understand that finding the right psychologist is an important decision. These are the questions I hear most often.

Book a Consultation
You don't need a GP referral to see me as a private patient. However, if you have a Mental Health Treatment Plan (MHTP) from your GP, you may be eligible for Medicare rebates on up to 10 sessions per calendar year. I'm happy to discuss what's best for your situation.
Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy (ISTDP) is an evidence-based approach that works directly with the unconscious emotional processes underlying psychological difficulties. It has strong empirical support for complex presentations including personality disorders, treatment-resistant depression, and anxiety disorders. Unlike generic CBT, it addresses root causes rather than managing symptoms.
The first session is a comprehensive assessment — typically 90 minutes — where I gain a thorough understanding of your history, current difficulties, and goals. It's also an opportunity for you to assess whether working with me feels right. There's no pressure to commit to ongoing sessions after our first meeting.
International sessions are conducted via a secure, encrypted telehealth platform. Sessions are available across most time zones — I work early mornings and evenings to accommodate clients in the UK, Europe, and North America. Payment is straightforward via international bank transfer or card.
My fees reflect my level of experience and specialist training. Standard 50-minute sessions are $280. Initial assessment sessions (90 minutes) are $420. Medicare rebates apply for patients with a valid Mental Health Treatment Plan — reducing the out-of-pocket cost significantly. I'm transparent about fees from the outset and happy to discuss options.
