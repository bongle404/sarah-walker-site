## AHPRA Constraints — Psychology

Psychology is one of the highest-sensitivity AHPRA areas.

- No clinical outcome claims ("helped with", "resolved", "treated successfully")
- No testimonials that reference specific symptoms or diagnoses
- Copy must be pain-led (client's situation first) not credential-led
- "Some people find..." framing for patient experience — never absolute claims
